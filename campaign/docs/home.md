---
title: War of the Dragon Banners
subtitle: A Pendragon campaign · Salisbury, 462–465
---

Vortigern is High King of Logres, and he has given the Saxons land. Hengist's people hold Kent. The
kings before him were murdered one after another, and the lords who rose against him with his son
Vortimer lost, and then lost again. In 463 Vortigern called the lords of Britain to a peace council at
the Giant's Dance on Salisbury Plain, and at the feast the Saxons drew hidden knives and killed some three
hundred of them. It is called the Night of the Long Knives.

The squires of this campaign are the children of rebels. Most of them were pages together at Sarum, the
great fortress of Salisbury, and most of their fathers died that night. What they were due to inherit
is held up by a king who does not forgive, and the Earl of Salisbury who should have confirmed it is dead,
his heir missing.

They were chosen as squires by six knights who call themselves the Wolves of Vagon, after the wolf pack
they hunted down in a cave when they were squires themselves. Now a young man named Merlin has come to the
Wolves with a dream: a banner that must reach its rightful bearer before winter, and a man who will reveal
himself once it does.
