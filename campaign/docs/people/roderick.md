The son of the late Earl of Salisbury, and so the Wolves' lord, though he is only a squire. He had been squired
to Sir Perrin, a knight of Cameliard, and went with him against Cambrian raiders beyond the dyke, where giants
took them both. Sineda cut him down in the giants' cave. He is now in Sir Hywel's charge, and Sir Elad does
not let him out of his sight.
