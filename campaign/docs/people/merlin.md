A thin, dark-haired young man, younger than the squires, whom the Wolves rescued from bandits in 465. He
dreams of a banner that must reach its rightful bearer before winter, and of a man who will reveal himself
when it does. He knows roughly where it lies, and he does not like delays. He says he is not one of the fair
folk. He said the brothers of Cameliard would kill each other and a cousin would rule.
