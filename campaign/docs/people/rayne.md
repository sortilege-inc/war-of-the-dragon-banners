The Red Giant of Wylye: a giant of a man with red hair and a red beard, of a family that can name its
ancestors back ten generations to the tribal kings who fought the Romans. To him the Saxons are only the latest
invaders. He is modest, up with the dawn, never tired, and the best swordsman of the Wolves; they look to him
in battle. He is the one open pagan among them, devoted to the bear goddess Artio, who he believes shielded
him from a mother bear as a boy. He puts up with the others' jokes about unwashed heathens, sits through mass,
and does not wear the cross. He claimed Lothwellen because the boy already understood that a knight must first
be a good man.
