Sir Clefford ap Codford, called Lord Clefford by his friends to tease him: his father was a knight banneret
with his own bannermen, and Clefford insisted on the title. The joke gets funnier now that he is due to
inherit it, because his father died at the Night of the Long Knives, and there is no Earl of Salisbury to
confirm him. His family is well established in western Salisbury and joined by blood and marriage to many
lineages across Logres, so he knows everyone and hears all the gossip.

He is married to the Lady Trisha of Tintagel, and their twins were christened last winter. After Sir Elad he
is the wealthiest of the Wolves, and it shows in his clothes and his house, where he entertains the others
as often as he can. He is always correctly turned out at court, likes a bawdy story in private, and is
famously reckless in a fight: he has never refused a charge. He killed an Irish chieftain and took his ransom.
Sir Hywel warns the squires not to talk of fairies around him.
