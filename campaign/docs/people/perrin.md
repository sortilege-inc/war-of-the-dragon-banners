A knight of Cameliard, to whom Roderick was squired. His last squire was taken by the Saxons and, the rumour
goes, boiled alive rather than ransomed. He led a company west against Cambrian raiders, was ambushed beyond
the dyke, and was taken by giants with a club-blow to the head. The squires found him hanging in the giants'
cave. He may never be quite right again.
