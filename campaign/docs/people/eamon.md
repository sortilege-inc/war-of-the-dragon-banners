The Fox. A household knight of Sir Elad's, with no land of his own: the King of Estregales knighted him, but
the knighthood came without a manor, and he came to Salisbury, as he says, for better friends and fortune
than Cambria gave him. He is the most charming man in Salisbury, or at least the best-looking. He plays the
harp, sings, and tells stories better than anyone, always about other men and never himself. He likes a
riddle. He trained Paun in awareness by ambushing him, and advised him to keep his own counsel.
