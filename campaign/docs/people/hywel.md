The finest spearman of the Wolves, on foot or on a charger. He is good-looking and a shameless flirt, and
the rumours link him to many women and to none for long. Alone of the Wolves he loves falconry, which the
others say is not proper hunting because the bird does the work; he raised his own hawk from the egg. He
rarely comes home from a hunt empty-handed, and he has the best hands among them for binding a wound. It was
Sir Hywel who kept Sineda alive after the boar. Roderick is in his charge.
