The lord of the castle of Vagon, which he inherited a year or two before the story begins, and the one
lord among the Wolves; he survived the Night of the Long Knives because he was not important enough to be
invited. He is of middling height and stocky, and so prematurely bald that he looks older than he is. He is
the quiet one, and keeps his own counsel, except in the drill yard, where he insists on hard training for
every man under him; he inherited the work of training knights from his father. When he says dawn he means
dawn. He claimed Sineda at his sickbed and expects him to live up to it.
