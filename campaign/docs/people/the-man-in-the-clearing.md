A man building himself a house of fallen timber in a clearing in the great forest between the kingdoms, where
the white hart led Paun and Sineda. He asked them a great many questions about knights, laws and oaths, and
sent them to the old Roman fort in the mountains, warning them that things are not always as they seem.
