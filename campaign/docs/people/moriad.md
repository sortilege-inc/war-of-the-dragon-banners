The Gorgon, so named by some Roman wit who was probably thrashed for it once Moriad understood the joke.
The Saxons burned his family's house; his father died of an axe to the head, and his mother of her injuries
after she carried him out. He grew up a ward at Vagon, and Sir Elad was his only friend until the Wolves took
him in. One side of his face and body is badly scarred. No man hates the Saxons more, Sir Elad says, and none
has more reason. He wears an open-faced helmet in battle so that his enemies can see him grin. At court he is
self-conscious, so he dresses well and dances surprisingly well. He is too proud to admit what he does not
know, and the other Wolves amuse themselves by daring him into things.
