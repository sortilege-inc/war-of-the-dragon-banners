Sineda's cousin, a Pict of Jagent who keeps the old gods. His uncles on his father's side are knights, and he
has four younger sisters and a younger brother. At the Midsummer feast he held the attention of a young lady
of good family all evening, and asked the Earl's leave to court her, and was told to come back when he was a
knight.

Drust has dreamed of the banner, more clearly than any of the others. He means to hold a straight line in a crooked world, and would
rather be a song than a statue. Lord Clefford is having him dressed properly, and taught to read.
