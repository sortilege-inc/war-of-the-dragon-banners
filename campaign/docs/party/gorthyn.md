A Belgae boy like Paun, their fathers old acquaintances. His father held Constans's court with a story of a
fair woman in the forest, and some people wonder whether the family has fairy blood. Gorthyn is proud, and
at the Midsummer feast he tried to impress Sir Rayne and spilled wine on him instead.
