From the Saxon Shore, where the Saxons land first. His father kept quiet for years and then joined the
rebellion anyway, fought beside Catigern at Aylesford, and died at the Night of the Long Knives.

Lothwellen was the most genial of the squires at the Midsummer feast, and the first through the quintain. On
his first hunt he swore to bring down the finest buck in the forest and did not, and on the second he put his
spear through a boar that kept coming. He keeps the other tusk. What he wants most from the Wolves is to learn
to be a good man, and to stay one. Sir Rayne claimed him for exactly that.
