A Cambrian from Powys, where one of Vortigern's grandsons rules under Vortigern's regency. His family lost its
lands in the rebellion. He wears the sign of a Cambrian war god, as his father did, and hopes to be half the
fighter his father was. He chose the right horse for the hunt, and rode the boar down twice.
