Tiphaine is fourteen, the daughter of Sir Riwall ap Hoel, a Breton knight who won a small manor in the
western marches of Salisbury and died fighting against Vortigern, and of Lady Marina Aureliana, of a
Romano-British family of the chalk downs. She reads and writes Latin, flies her own goshawk, Argantia, and
keeps her father's hunting knife at her belt. Her uncle in Armorica had her squired by letter. She arrived at
Sarum with two saddlebags and a hooded hawk on her fist.

She has not yet come into the story.
