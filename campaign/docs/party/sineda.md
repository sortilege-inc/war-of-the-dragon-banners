The smallest of the squires, and the one with the most extraordinary luck. His grandfather died among the
Pictish bodyguards Vortigern beheaded for King Constans's murder, and his father, a rebel who heard Vortimer's
last words at Ebbsfleet, died at the Night of the Long Knives. He carries the family's jewelled sword, and he
hates giants. He speaks with a stutter, and he can read and write Gaelic. He would rather fight with a stick.

He struck the quintain so squarely the weight passed over his head, left the Midsummer feast with Jenny, and
was nearly killed by a boar; Sir Elad claimed him as his own squire at his sickbed, and the waters of
Glastonbury healed him. He keeps the boar's tusk. He has four younger brothers and a sister, and means to do
right by them.
